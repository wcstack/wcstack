synth playground — a synthesizer made of HTML tags

Nesting is the signal chain (signal flows downward); anything nesting can't express is routed by id (out="bus", param="frequency"). Play with the on-screen keys, your computer keyboard (A–; = notes, WET… = sharps, Z/X = octave), or a MIDI keyboard. View source: the page is the patch.

Built from @wcstack/audio and @wcstack/midi. The keyboard and the scope are this demo's own components — wcstack I/O nodes carry no rendering, so drawing is the page's job. See the README. Audio starts on your first click or key press.
